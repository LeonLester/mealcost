Coach LeonidasMeal cost calculator

Get the app

Meal cost works in any browser at mealcost.coachleonidas.com. You can also install it on your phone. It is free and open source.

Android

Download the APK

Open the file on your phone and allow installs from your browser when asked. New versions install over the old one and keep your data.

Android, with automatic updates

Add the Coach Leonidas repository to the free F-Droid app and updates arrive on their own.

Add the F-Droid repository

iPhone and iPad

Open the site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen like an app and works offline.
