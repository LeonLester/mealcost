Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026. The Meal cost calculator at mealcost.coachleonidas.com is a free tool run by Coach Leonidas (coachleonidas.com).

Without signing in

Your Cronometer export, prices and settings are processed in your browser and stored in your browser's local storage. They are not sent to our servers. Clearing your browser data, or using "Delete all data from this browser" on the Prices tab, removes them.

Barcode lookups

If you use the barcode lookup when adding a food, only the barcode number is sent, to Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org) and, if it is not found there, to USDA FoodData Central (api.nal.usda.gov). The camera image stays on your device. Those services have their own privacy terms. Nutrition data from Open Food Facts is available under the Open Database License.

If you use Typical prices, only the food name you type and your currency are sent to the meal cost server, which asks Open Food Facts and Open Prices (prices.openfoodfacts.org) and, for US dollars, the US Bureau of Labor Statistics (api.bls.gov). Nothing is stored or linked to you. Open Prices data is available under the Open Database License.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional and only used to sync your data between your devices.

  • From Google we receive your email address and a Google account identifier. We do not request any other Google data, and we do not read your contacts, mail, files or calendar.
  • We store your email address, the identifier, and one copy of your calculator data: food log entries, nutrient values, prices, offers, recipes and categories.
  • We use this only to run the sync. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or use it for analytics or profiling.
  • A sign-in cookie keeps you signed in for 30 days. It is the only cookie we set. We use no tracking or advertising cookies.

Where data is stored

The synced copy is stored with Cloudflare, which hosts this site and its database. Cloudflare also processes ordinary request data such as IP addresses to deliver the site. Google processes your sign-in under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

On the Prices tab, choose "Delete my account and cloud data" while signed in. This removes your account, your sign-in sessions and the synced copy straight away. Data stored in your browser is not affected and can be removed separately. Signing out stops syncing and keeps the data in this browser.

Children

The service is not directed at children under 16.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes. Questions or deletion requests can be sent through coachleonidas.com.
